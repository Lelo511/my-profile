Background

Family: Raised in a household where we fixed things ourselves. No money for new stuff meant I learned to troubleshoot, adapt, and make do. That’s now my debugging style: no excuses, just solutions.

Inspiration & Motivation

I’m inspired by township businesses running R100k/month off a notebook and sheer hustle. They deserve the same tech as big companies. My motivation is to bridge that gap.

Long term, I want freedom: to work from anywhere, build products that pay me while I sleep, and prove that world-class devs can come from anywhere — including Chartwell.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Strengths: Relentless problem-solving. I’ll debug for 6 hours straight if needed. I ship fast, design for mobile first, and understand what SA users actually need — not just what looks good in California.

Weaknesses: I over-polish. I’ll spend an hour adjusting a button when I should ship the feature. I’m fixing this by using 90-minute sprints and launching MVPs. Still growing my back-end skills beyond Firebase.

Why SEO & Web Development

Web dev lets me build once and help thousands. SEO makes sure people actually find it. Most SA devs can code. Few can rank. I want both skills because a beautiful site with zero traffic is just expensive art.

For local businesses, page 1 of Google is life or death. If I can build the site AND get it ranked, I become 10x more valuable than a basic coder.

Where I See Myself in 5 Years

Goal: Prove that you don’t need to be in Sandton or Cape Town to build global-level tech. Chartwell can ship too.
